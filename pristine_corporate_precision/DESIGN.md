## Brand & Style

This design system embodies corporate authority, hygiene precision, and forward-looking African enterprise vigor. Built for premier commercial clients across Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Ikeja, and Lekki, the visual tone bridges uncompromising operational dependability with the effortless hospitality and freshness demanded by luxury shortlet managers and prime facility developers.

The design movement combines **Modern Corporate** reliability with **Pristine Atmospheric Minimalism**:
- **Generous Whitespace & Crisp Clarity:** Vast breathing room evoking sanitized, orderly corporate interiors.
- **Controlled Vibrancy:** Authority-rich deep oceanic navy anchors the composition, lifted by vibrant emerald freshness and high-energy cyan interaction cues.
- **Refined Tactility:** Soft, atmospheric elevation, hairline borders, and fluid responsive modules that feel dependable and instantaneous on high-end desktop workstations as well as field-ready mobile devices.

## Colors

The palette establishes clinical order paired with vibrant energy:

- **Primary (`#0A2540`):** Deep Oceanic Navy. Used for core brand identity, primary navigation headers, dominant buttons, and institutional anchor points. Imparts corporate authority, permanence, and professional rigor.
- **Secondary (`#0E9F6E`):** Emerald / Vitality Green. Signifies spotless hygiene, eco-friendly chemical standards, active facility readiness, and verified inspection badges.
- **Tertiary / Action Accent (`#00C3F8`):** Crisp Cyan. Used for high-engagement focal points, interactive highlights, active states, progress indicators, and localized fintech touchpoints.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate-900. High-legibility text baseline for headlines, critical data labels, and microcopy. Secondary copy maps strictly to Slate-600 (`#475569`).
- **Neutral Surface / Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) as the baseline canvas, supported by tiered cool frost layers: Surface-Subtle (`#F8FAFC`) and Surface-Muted (`#F1F5F9`) for modular separation and card backdrops.
- **Functional Accents:** Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`) serves exclusively for caution banners, SLAs, and priority maintenance callouts. WhatsApp Emerald (`#25D366`) is reserved strictly for immediate concierge booking triggers.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Instrument Sans** for clean, confident headlines and labels with **Inter** for clinical, data-dense legibility across operational tables, pricing matrices, and facility service specs.

- **Headings (Instrument Sans):** Use medium-to-bold weights with restrained negative tracking for a confident, contemporary character. Numbers and metric readouts should leverage tabular figures when displaying square footage or currency figures (NGN ₦).
- **Body & Longform (Inter):** Regular 400 weight with relaxed line heights ensures high readability during multi-step quotation workflows and facility audit logs.
- **Labels & Micro-Tokens (Instrument Sans):** Employ medium-to-bold weights with uppercase treatment for pill metadata, status tags, and operational dispatch counters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered around a fluid 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, resolving to a 4-column structure on mobile devices:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, max-width container capped at `1280px`, with `margin-lg` (80px) and `gutter-lg` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, full-bleed canvas with `margin-md` (48px) and `gutter` (24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `margin` (24px) down to 16px safe areas and `gutter-sm` (16px).

Rhythm adheres to a strict 8pt base grid. Component paddings leverage `space-md` for compact utility elements and `space-xl` through `space-2xl` for executive cards, onboarding sections, and quote calculators.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is built on an **Atmospheric Cleanliness** model that pairs crisp hairline borders with tinted, multi-layered diffuse shadows:

- **Base Surfaces (Level 0):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and Slate Frost `#F8FAFC` background planes with no drop shadow.
- **Hairline Dividers & Enclosures:** Thin `1px` structural borders in `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` (Slate-900 at 8% opacity) maintaining spatial precision without heaviness.
- **Card & Tier Elevation (Level 1):** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.05)`, bordered by `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Delivers a floating, pristine surface for pricing plans and property categories.
- **Active / Popover / Sticky Nav (Level 2):** Frosted glass baseline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) accompanied by `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08)` and a bottom border in `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Floating Interactive Trigger (WhatsApp / Emergency Clean):** Level 3 prominence featuring `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(14, 159, 110, 0.35)` using a directional emerald tint to communicate instant responsiveness.

## Shapes

The design system uses a deliberate, rounded geometry (level 2) that softens corporate rigidity while conveying modern architectural aesthetics:

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Form inputs, tooltips, list row selections, table badges.
- **Cards & Content Modules (1rem / 16px to 1.5rem / 24px):** Facility feature cards, client review blocks, quotation breakdowns, and dashboard panels make prominent use of `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem) to reinforce premium touch and feel.
- **Pills (Full Radius / 9999px):** Status badges ("Verified Sanitized", "Post-Construction Ready"), primary CTA button sets, chips, and the floating live booking bubble.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Deep Oceanic Navy (`#0A2540`) background with Crisp White text. Fully rounded pill (`rounded-full`) or `rounded-xl`, `h-12`, medium weight Instrument Sans text. Subtle hover state shifting to Navy-800 (`#071C33`) with a crisp micro-lift (`translate-y-[-1px]`).
- **Accent Action:** Emerald Green (`#0E9F6E`) background with Crisp White text. Reserved for primary conversion flows ("Book Deep Clean", "Instant Inspection").
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1px` border in `#E2E8F0`, slate-900 text, shifting to frost-white background (`#F8FAFC`) on hover.
- **Global CTA hierarchy (meeting decision, 23 Oct 2026 at 00:40:29; wording refined 4 Nov 2026):** "Book a service" is the only persistent primary action in the header. It covers both cleaning and facility-support bookings without awkward phrasing. Do not place a full "Chat on WhatsApp" button beside it. WhatsApp remains available as the circular floating contact control and may appear contextually where conversation is required, such as a custom-quote state.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Height `28px`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `px-3.5`.
- **Active / Clean:** Emerald tint background (`rgba(14, 159, 110, 0.1)`) with `#0E9F6E` text and an integrated 6px glowing green status pip.
- **Pending / Scheduled:** Amber tint background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`) with `#D97706` text.
- **Enterprise SLA:** Cyan tint background (`rgba(0, 195, 248, 0.12)`) with `#0284C7` text.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Crisp pure white container (`#FFFFFF`) with `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`) corners and a hairline `1px` border in Slate-100 (`#F1F5F9`).
- Internal padding using `space-lg` (24px) for mobile and `space-xl` (40px) for desktop presentation.
- Interactive cards feature a smooth border transition to Cyan (`#00C3F8`) and a delicate upward shadow translation upon hover.

### Audience Image Bento
- The homepage audience module uses a 12-column, asymmetric `7/5` then `5/7` editorial grid. Each card is image-led, with a navy lower gradient ensuring accessible white copy over photography.
- Images should show the setting and work context, not decorative stock abstractions. Keep the supporting copy short enough to remain readable over the photograph and preserve one obvious contextual action per card.
- On mobile, collapse to one card per row with a minimum height of `330px`; retain the image, icon, title, short description and action rather than turning the cards back into generic text tiles.

### Proof of Clean / Handover Record
- Treat quality control as a visible product feature. The signature handover module pairs a concise benefit statement with a white operational-record card on an emerald-tinted field.
- The record card may show arrival context, completed areas, completion checks, access notes and preferences. When sample imagery is used, label the module as an example; never imply that illustrative or generated imagery is a client case study.
- This pattern explains the existing service process. It must not be represented as a paid add-on or separate service.

### Booking Journey
- Summarize the public journey in three customer-facing stages: `Choose & price`, `Confirm & pay`, and `Team & handover`.
- Use three equal cards on desktop and a single vertical stack on mobile. Avoid exposing internal operational steps as separate journey cards unless the customer must act on them.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Height `48px`, `rounded-xl` geometry, background `#FFFFFF` bordered by `#CBD5E1`.
- Font: Inter 15px.
- Focus State: Border color switches crisply to `#0A2540` accompanied by a 3px ring in `rgba(0, 195, 248, 0.2)`. Never use aggressive browser defaults.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px`, `rounded-md` for checkboxes and `rounded-full` for radios.
- Bordered in `#94A3B8`. Checked state fills instantly with Emerald (`#0E9F6E`) featuring a white vector checkmark.

### Navigation & Header
- Sticky top navigation with frosted translucency (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` blur 16px).
- Incorporates location/hub indicators ("Serving Victoria Island, Lekki & Ikeja"), enterprise quote trigger, and customer phone direct line.

### Floating Urgent Concierge / WhatsApp Trigger
- Fixed position: Bottom right (`bottom-6 right-6`), z-index 50.
- Diameter `56px` circular control with WhatsApp brand Emerald (`#25D366`), a white icon, accessible label/tooltip, and distinct ambient shadow (`0px 12px 28px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.38)`). Keep it icon-only at every breakpoint so it does not compete with the booking CTA.
