# Kleandeck website content and structure brief

**Decision source:** website discussion from the 23 October 2026 meeting, through 00:43:43.  
**Excluded:** the later AI configurator/product discussion. It is not part of this website scope.

## 1. Website objective

Position Kleandeck as a dependable B2B commercial cleaning and operational-support partner. The site must help a non-technical buyer quickly understand:

1. what Kleandeck does;
2. whether Kleandeck fits their type of business or project;
3. what the starting price covers;
4. what happens from booking through handover; and
5. why the team can be trusted inside a client space.

The primary conversion is **Book a service** (wording refined on 4 November 2026). A floating WhatsApp icon remains available for questions and custom quotes, but does not compete with the primary action in the header.

## 2. Priority audiences and use cases

- **Interior designers and project teams:** post-construction and handover cleans after installations.
- **Gyms and high-traffic facilities:** scheduled cleaning and facility support shaped around long operating hours.
- **Offices needing weekend deep cleaning:** a reset before Monday without disrupting weekday operations.
- **Small businesses and startups:** a combined facilities-assistant offer covering cleaning, pantry care, room resets and agreed basic office support.
- **Job candidates:** people seeking a career in professional cleaning or facility support, routed to Careers and the Academy.

Do not make Lagos the main landing-page message. Local service-area information can support pricing, contact and local SEO without narrowing the core value proposition.

## 3. Positioning and language

### Core statement

**Reliable cleaning and facility support for growing businesses.**

Supporting copy should explain that Kleandeck combines vetted people, clear communication and an accountable handover. Use plain operational language rather than lifestyle-only cleaning copy.

### Service language

- Standard cleaning
- Deep cleaning
- Post-construction cleaning
- Facility/office support for ongoing business needs

The combined cleaner and office-assistant offer should be described as a **facilities assistant** or **office support** service. Its scope can include cleaning, pantry care, meeting-room resets and other agreed practical support. Avoid implying an unlimited role; every placement needs a defined scope.

### Trust language

Replace generic "trained cleaners" emphasis with the fuller safety promise:

- two weeks of practical training;
- screening and background/identity verification where applicable;
- vetted assignment to help protect the client's space and belongings; and
- internal quality control before handover.

## 4. Information architecture

### Primary navigation

- Services — links to the homepage service section
- Pricing
- About us
- Join Kleandeck — grouped control containing:
  - Careers
  - Academy
- Book a service — the only persistent primary header CTA

FAQs live on the landing page. The old `/faq` route should redirect to the homepage FAQ section so existing links continue to work without maintaining a separate FAQ destination.

### Global contact behavior

- Use a 56 px circular floating WhatsApp icon on all breakpoints.
- Provide an accessible label and tooltip.
- Do not place a full WhatsApp button beside Book a service.
- Contextual WhatsApp actions are still appropriate for custom quotes or contact-specific states.

## 5. Homepage structure

### Hero

The hero must be SEO-clear and business-first:

- eyebrow: Commercial cleaning & operational support
- H1: Reliable cleaning and facility support for growing businesses.
- summary: weekend deep cleans, everyday office support, vetted crew, clear communication and accountable handover
- primary CTA: Book a service
- secondary CTA: Explore services
- proof points: vetted team, photo-verified handover, clear core-service pricing

Audience selection belongs in the body, not in the hero.

### Who we support

Use four explicit cards for interior-design partners, gyms/active facilities, offices needing a reset, and startups/small businesses. Each card should explain the operational use case and have a descriptive link.

### Facilities assistant / office support

Give the combined offer its own section. Explain the benefit to lean teams and the boundaries of an agreed scope. Link to the booking/quote journey.

### Before and after

- Label the represented areas, including restrooms.
- Keep before/after controls and labels legible on mobile.
- On real projects, explain that pre- and post-service photos form part of the internal quality process.
- Do not present quality assurance as a separate paid service.

### Services and pricing

Show standard, deep and post-construction cleaning as the core bookable cleaning services. State that the quote calculator covers the selected core service. Extras and site requirements can change the final price and are confirmed before work begins.

Examples of separate site needs include water provision/a water tanker, unusual access, specialist equipment and requested add-ons.

### Booking journey

Show the full sequence:

1. choose a service;
2. review the core-service estimate;
3. review and accept the terms;
4. pay securely;
5. receive the assigned team and arrival confirmation; and
6. receive a photo-verified handover, with preferences recorded for future visits.

The booking flow already includes a required Terms and Conditions checkbox. Preserve it.

### QA and security

QA is an internal operating process, not another service:

- capture pre-service context where needed;
- capture post-service photos;
- review photos before keys are returned/handover is completed; and
- record client preferences and access notes for repeat visits.

Security messaging should connect training with screening and vetting, not imply that training alone protects client property.

### FAQs

Publish the full FAQ set on the landing page, covering service scope, pricing, extras/site needs, payment, rescheduling and day-of-service expectations.

### Careers and Academy

End the homepage with a combined Careers & Academy pathway. Careers speaks to job opportunities. Academy explains screening and the basics of professional cleaning/workplace conduct/facility support.

## 6. Pricing rules

- Display prices as starting thresholds or estimates for core services.
- Show VAT clearly.
- Do not claim the calculator is a guaranteed final price when the site has additional needs.
- Confirm extras and site-specific requirements before assigning the team or beginning work.
- Large sites, facility staffing and non-standard scopes move to a custom quote.

## 7. Terms and refund policy

The agreed policy from the meeting is:

- payment is non-refundable after a booking is confirmed; and
- the exception is when Kleandeck fails to attend the agreed booking, in which case the client may choose a reschedule or refund.

This policy must be visible in the terms reviewed before payment. Final contact details remain pending and should not be invented.

## 8. About us page

The About page should sound human and specific. It should introduce Kleandeck as the practical partner behind a space that works, then evidence four values:

- clear communication;
- quality that can be verified;
- client details/preferences remembered; and
- vetted, trained people clients can trust in their space.

Avoid generic values words without operating proof.

## 9. SEO architecture

### Site-wide title and description themes

Use commercial cleaning, facility support, deep cleaning, post-construction cleaning, office support, gyms, interior projects and growing businesses. Keep Lagos/location modifiers in relevant service-area and pricing copy rather than leading every page.

### Page intent

- `/` — commercial cleaning and facility support; audiences and use cases
- `/pricing` — commercial cleaning starting prices, quote scope and cost factors
- `/about` — Kleandeck process, communication, vetting and QA
- `/careers` — professional cleaning and facility-support jobs
- `/academy` — screening and cleaning/facility-support training basics
- `/book` — transactional service-selection and payment journey
- `/faq` — redirect to `/#faq`

### Content principles

- One descriptive H1 per page.
- Use descriptive headings and links; avoid vague "learn more" labels.
- Describe image purpose in alt text when the image conveys information.
- Keep service names consistent between navigation, marketing copy and booking data.
- Add structured data only after final business contact details and service areas are confirmed.
